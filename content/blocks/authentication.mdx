### Composition

The block presents sign-in, six-digit verification, and recovery as three focused views inside one stable frame. Labels, autocomplete hints, focus order, and native submission semantics are included.

### Integration boundary

Use `onSubmit` to connect authentication requests and `onViewChange` for analytics or routing. Credential verification, session creation, rate limiting, and recovery delivery remain backend responsibilities.
